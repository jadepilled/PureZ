About

Hardcore vanilla+ DayZ. A small set of mods, each one there for a reason. The jank is fixed. The balance stays vanilla.

Features

Gunplay

Reworked handling and recoil. Vanilla weapons only, plus extra scopes, magazines, universal suppressors and lasers.

Loot

More of it. Guns spawn worn or better, often with attachments and nearly always with sights. Mags come loaded on the gun, at least 75% full.

Infected

Far more zombies. Dynamic Mi-17 and UH-1Y crash sites. No AI missions.

Map

Nasdara first, with more maps to come.

No HUD

Weapon info only. The hotbar shows in your inventory. Hold H to check your status monitor.

Respawn

Set your spawn at a sleeping bag. One hour before you can set it again.

Groups

Play with your squad, built in.

Quality of life

Fixes for the small things that get in the way. Nothing that changes how DayZ plays.

Vehicles

More spawns. Every vehicle spawns pristine, fuelled and complete. Code locks and garage storage. 3PP while driving.

Play
