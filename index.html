Features

PvP

Our unique, fast-paced gunplay system blends modern shooter mechanics with fluid movement to deliver an unforgettable experience.

Flight System

Custom flight system based on real flight simulation, with 4 helicopters and 3 planes spawning in fitting locations throughout our maps.

Dynamic Events

Dynamic crashes, infected hoardes, and keycard missions. Get lost in immersive events that seamlessly integrate with the game.

No HUD

We prioritise immersion, with a modified extremely minimal HUD. An in-game Status Monitor becomes your stat readout: check it by holding H.

Custom UI

Sleek, minimal UI that simplifies the experience and makes your gameplay better when it counts. Easy to read and out of the way when you don't need it.

Infected

Infected are widespread, and you may find them easier to deal with individually. They can be quite a problem in groups, not to mention hoardes.

Groups

Get your squad on comms and band together in our Groups system. Press J at any time to open the groups menu and invite up to 12 friends.

Quality of life

There are too many minor fixes to mention here. Each one addresses an existing problem, and nothing changes how DayZ fundamentally plays.

Play

Search PureZ in the DayZ launcher or server browser. Only one workshop mod to install: PureZ.
